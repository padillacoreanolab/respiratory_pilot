How to review

Click a green peak to remove it (red ×); click it again to restore it. Shift+click or right-click on a missed breath to add a peak (orange); it snaps to the highest point within ±30 ms. Click an orange peak to delete it. ←/→ previous/next page, ↑/↓ zoom, Ctrl+Z or U undo, S save. Click or drag the overview strip to jump.

Full review: the recording is split into consecutive 5 s pages, and you look at every one. On each page, fix every peak between the dashed lines (remove wrong ones, add missed breaths, mark anything unreadable as unusable), then press Space to mark the page reviewed and move to the next. N jumps to the next page not yet reviewed. The strip under the overview turns green as pages are reviewed. Judge each cycle from the trace, not from whether a marker is already there. Press Space again on a reviewed page to un-mark it.

Video: the cagemate video plays above the trace, matched to it through the camera's frame timestamps. The red line on the trace marks the video's current moment. P (or Play this page) plays the current page and stops at its end. While paused, moving the mouse over the trace moves the video to that time, so you can see what the mouse and tubing were doing during a stretch before judging it.

Nothing is masked automatically. The whole recording is shown and searched for peaks. You decide what is unusable: press E (or Mark unusable) and drag across a stretch with no readable breathing. It turns gray, its peaks fade and stop counting, and no breath interval crosses it. In that mode, click a gray stretch to remove it; U undoes. Small but regular cycles are breaths: keep them.

Automatic flags (off by default; tick Show automatic flags): red = very large spikes (>20 robust SD, e.g. air pushes), purple = low amplitude (<20% of the 2-minute rolling median for ≥1 s). They only point to places worth a look and never remove anything. G/Shift+G jumps between purple flags. The overview's lower lane is breath amplitude (log scale), with that 20% line dashed.

Edits are kept in this browser automatically, but only Save writes files: _reviewed_peaks.csv and _peak_review.json go to your Downloads folder. Move them into manual_peak_review/saved_reviews/. To resume on another computer, use Load review JSON. Changing the floor keeps your edits.
